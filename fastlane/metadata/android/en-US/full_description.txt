Niko Clock tells the time out loud. It's made for people who can't read the screen easily: people who can't read, older people, and people with low vision.

What it does:
* Shows the time and date in large text
* Tap the clock or the big button to hear the time. Tap twice for the date
* Says the time by itself every hour, half hour or quarter hour, quiet at night
* Shake the phone to hear the time, even with the screen off
* A day clock mode that shows the day, morning or evening, and the full date in large words
* Can show the Nepali date and a short note from a helper, and say good morning with the date
* Quick settings tiles, and time, date, or time and date widgets
* Speaks in natural sentences in English, Nepali, Hindi, Bengali, Urdu, Spanish, Portuguese, French and Indonesian, using the phone's own voice
* A short setup for helpers, and settings protected by a PIN
* Warns when a phone setting may stop it speaking

Niko Clock has no ads, no tracking and no internet permission. It is free and open source under the GPLv3.

Niko Clock is a one-person side project. Much of the code is written with AI assistance, then reviewed and tested on real phones.
