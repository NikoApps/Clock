Niko Clock tells the time out loud. It's made for people who can't read the screen easily: people who can't read, older people, and people with low vision.

What it does:
* Shows the time and date in large text
* Tap the clock or the big button to hear the time. Tap twice for the date
* Says the time by itself every hour, half hour or quarter hour, quiet at night
* Shake the phone to hear the time, even with the screen off
* Quick settings tiles and a home-screen widget
* Speaks in natural sentences in English, Nepali, Hindi, Spanish and Portuguese, using the phone's own voice
* Settings for helpers, protected by a PIN

Niko Clock has no ads, no tracking and no internet permission. It is free and open source under the GPLv3.
