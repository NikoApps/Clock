Niko Clock tells the time out loud. It's made for people who can't read the screen easily: people who can't read, older people, and people with low vision.

What it does now:
* Shows the time in very large numbers
* Speaks the time when you press one big button
* Speaks in your phone's language, using the phone's own voice

Coming soon:
* Speak the time when you shake the phone
* Hourly announcements with quiet hours at night
* The date, including local calendars

Niko Clock has no ads, no tracking and no internet permission. It is free and open source under the GPLv3.
